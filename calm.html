Questions, answered

I've tried meditation before and my mind just wanders. Will this actually work for me?+

Calm is specifically designed for people who describe themselves as having a busy brain. The guided meditations give your mind something gentle to follow so wandering thoughts naturally settle. Many people notice a difference from their very first session.

I don't have time for long sessions — is there anything short enough for a packed schedule?+

The app includes sessions ranging from just a few minutes upward, so you can fit a genuine wind-down into even the tightest evening. Daily offerings are curated with working professionals in mind. Consistency matters more than duration.

What exactly is a Sleep Story and how is it different from a podcast or audiobook?+

Sleep Stories are purpose-built audio experiences with pacing, narration tone, and sound design calibrated to slow your nervous system down as they unfold. Unlike podcasts they are not meant to be finished — the goal is sleep, not content. They work alongside the sleep meditations as part of a broader bedtime toolkit.

What does the free trial include and will I be charged automatically?+

The free trial gives you access to Calm's content so you can explore sleep stories, guided meditation, and daily programmes before deciding. You are in control of your subscription and can cancel before any charge applies. Full details are shown clearly at the point of sign-up.
