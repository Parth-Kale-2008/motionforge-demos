Questions, answered

Do I need any coding or design experience to build my site?+

None at all. Squarespace is built so that anyone can create a polished, professional website using drag-and-drop editing and AI-assisted setup. If you can type and click, you can build your site.

Can I sell products or services directly from my Squarespace website?+

Yes. The platform includes a full online store builder with product listings, secure checkout, and payment processing. You can also sell services, accept bookings, and send invoices from the same dashboard.

What happens after my free trial ends — am I locked into anything?+

Your trial lets you build and explore the platform at no cost. When you're ready to go live and accept payments or connect a custom domain, you choose a plan that fits your budget. There is no obligation during the trial period.

Is Squarespace a good fit for a small local business in Toronto?+

Absolutely. Squarespace is designed for small business owners who need a credible online presence quickly. You can showcase your services, collect leads, sell locally or nationally, and manage clients — all from one platform.
